## 2. Dịch vụ

### 2.1 Local SEO / Google Maps ([nguồn](https://sami-agency.de/local-seo/), DE: https://sami-agency.de/de/lokale-suchmaschinenoptimierung/)
- Hero: "Boost your revenue with targeted Local SEO strategies" / DE: "Steigern Sie Ihren Umsatz mit gezielten lokalen SEO-Strategien".
- Thông điệp: Local SEO giúp xuất hiện trong tìm kiếm "near me" / „In meiner Nähe" "exactly when customers are ready to book or visit"; "turns online searches into real customers – driving more bookings, more walk-ins, and more consistent revenue."
- Hạng mục công việc (nguyên văn EN):
  1. **Google Business Profile**: "Fully optimize and manage your Google Business Profile" · "Enhance it with services, high-quality images, and a Q&A section" · "Publish Google Posts and update images regularly" · "Monitor and remove spam competitors when needed".
  2. **Website & content**: fix technical SEO issues, structure + internal linking, landing pages mới, analytics tracking.
  3. **Location portfolio / citations**: listings quốc gia, địa phương, ngành; "Sync and protect your listings across Apple Maps, Bing Places, and Facebook."
  4. **Evaluation (review) management**: "Increase average star rating..." · "Monitor and respond to reviews on Google and Facebook." · tăng số review · hiển thị review trên website.
  5. **Backlinks**: "We never buy links or use link networks that Google has a negative opinion of, so your website is never at risk."
  6. **Monitoring & reporting**: dashboard, "customer portal", "Monthly video review meeting with your Account Manager".
- Bản DE có thêm: "30–60 relevante Keywords (einschließlich Gerichte, Standorte und Aktionen wie „bestellen" oder „reservieren")"; GBP: cập nhật giờ mở cửa, ảnh món + nội thất, upload Speisekarte, thu thập review.
- Ranking factors liệt kê: Customer reviews, GBP, NAP accuracy, Domain authority, Quality of citations, CTR, Keywords, UX mobile/desktop, Behavioral signals ("clicks, calls, direction requests").
- Con số trên trang (KHÔNG khuyến nghị dùng, xem mục 8): "$26,000,000 USD Worth of revenue generated, and ongoing!", "100+ Clients from referral alone", "100% Success!", "over 10 years of experience".
- Giá Local SEO: **không công bố riêng**. (Gói Social Media Standard/Premium có "Google Maps SEO", xem 2.6.)
- Lỗi template: FAQ nhắc "Singapore" (bản EN và DE) → đừng trích FAQ.
